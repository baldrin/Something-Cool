# 03 Colors

The group chose a hierarchy that is **true to the books** (decision D2 in [00 Decisions](00-decisions.md)): Golds and Obsidians really are physically stronger, and other Colors get different edges instead of equal power. This doc shows how that can still give every Color a reason to be in every crew.

**About the standing groups.** "High," "middle" and "low" are our summary of how the books treat each Color. Correct this doc wherever the books say otherwise.

**About the edge names.** Each Color has a **signature edge** with a working name, such as *Unseen* or *Command*. These are proposals for what the edge does, not final rules. The final mechanics depend on the dice core.

## Proposal: how the hierarchy works in the rules

Five ideas, to be tested together.

1. **Might.** Raw physical power comes in tiers. Golds and Obsidians sit at the top, Grays are trained above the base, and most other Colors are at the base. Might affects strength, speed and how much punishment a body takes. It does **not** affect skill, nerve or cleverness.
2. **Station.** Your Color sets your Station. Station decides where you can go openly, who has to listen to you, and how others treat you. A Gold can walk into any room. A Red cannot walk into most of them.
3. **Glory.** Renown is separate from Station and anyone can earn it. A Red with high Glory is still a Red to the law, but crowds know their name. (This is borrowed from Legend of the Five Rings. See [02 Borrowed mechanics](02-borrowed-mechanics.md).)
4. **Unseen and Watched.** High Station draws attention. In spaces ruled by highColors, lowColors in their expected roles are hardly noticed. Their sneaking, listening and first strikes get an edge there. Golds get the opposite: they are always watched, so they struggle to act quietly.
5. **Duty.** The more Station you have, the more is expected of you. Golds owe their House, Grays owe their chain of command, and Whites owe their doctrine. Duty can be called on at the worst possible time.

The intended effect: **Golds win when things are loud and open. lowColors win when things are quiet and hidden. Middle Colors win when expertise decides the outcome.** Most good operations need all three.

## The Colors

Each entry lists the Color's standing, what it is bred or trained for, its edge in and out of combat, its cost, and the scene types from [04 Scene types](04-scene-types.md) where it leads.

### Gold
- **Standing:** high, the ruling Color.
- **Bred and trained for:** rule, war and command. The best of them pass through the Institute and carry its scar.
- **In combat:** the strongest duelists and commanders. Top Might tier. Trained with razors, pulseFists and starShells.
- **Out of combat:** authority. Can enter almost any space, give orders to lower Colors, and stand in the politics of Houses and the Senate.
- **Cost:** Duty to their House, rivals who want them dead, and Watched: they can hardly go anywhere quietly. lowColors hide things from them.
- **Signature edge:** *Command.* Give an order that a lower Color must answer, or spend an action to grant an ally a better position.
- **Leads in:** Battle, Duel, Gold society.

### Silver
- **Standing:** high.
- **Bred and trained for:** money, trade and logistics.
- **In combat:** little personal fighting. Can pay for mercenaries, supplies or a way out.
- **Out of combat:** knows who owes whom, moves money, writes contracts, and reads markets for what Houses are planning.
- **Cost:** Golds see them as useful tools, and others see them as greedy. Physically at the base Might tier.
- **Signature edge:** *Debt.* Call in a favor owed to you to produce a resource, a contact or an opening.
- **Leads in:** Negotiation, Downtime.

### White
- **Standing:** high.
- **Bred and trained for:** faith, judgment and philosophy.
- **In combat:** little personal fighting. Their presence can give a fight legitimacy or call for a halt.
- **Out of combat:** law, ceremony and moral authority. A White's judgment can make an act lawful or unlawful in the eyes of Society.
- **Cost:** bound by doctrine. Their neutrality is their power, so taking sides openly can cost them everything.
- **Signature edge:** *Judgment.* Declare the terms of a dispute, trial or duel so that all sides who respect Society must honor them.
- **Leads in:** Court and ceremony, Rally.

### Copper
- **Standing:** high.
- **Bred and trained for:** bureaucracy, administration and law.
- **In combat:** none personally, but papers and permits can move troops, close a port or open a gate.
- **Out of combat:** records, permits and procedure. Can make a person disappear on paper or appear out of nowhere.
- **Cost:** seen as dull and rule bound. Their work leaves records that others can audit.
- **Signature edge:** *Paper Trail.* Create, find or erase an official record.
- **Leads in:** Court and ceremony, Investigation.

### Blue
- **Standing:** middle.
- **Bred and trained for:** piloting, navigation and ship systems.
- **In combat:** rules ship battles, flies ripWings and torchShips, and gets the crew in and out.
- **Out of combat:** navigation, orbital paths, sensors and long voyages. Thinks in cold logic.
- **Cost:** built for space more than for heavy gravity, and often seen as distant or unfeeling.
- **Signature edge:** *Link.* Act through a ship's systems as if the ship were your own body.
- **Leads in:** Ship battle, Travel and survival.

### Yellow
- **Standing:** middle.
- **Bred and trained for:** medicine, science and research, including the study of the mind.
- **In combat:** keeps people alive, treats wounds, and uses drugs to push allies further.
- **Out of combat:** medicine, research and analysis. Reads bodies and minds, which makes them strong investigators and questioners.
- **Cost:** base Might tier. Often used by Golds for work they find troubling.
- **Signature edge:** *Triage.* Put a fallen ally back in the fight, or learn a hidden truth from a body or a mind.
- **Leads in:** Investigation, Downtime.

### Green
- **Standing:** middle.
- **Bred and trained for:** technology, code and data.
- **In combat:** hacks pulseShields, turrets, drones and doors, and jams or spoofs communications.
- **Out of combat:** security systems, surveillance, data and digital identities.
- **Cost:** powerless where systems are cut off. Their work leaves traces that other Greens can follow.
- **Signature edge:** *Back Door.* Leave a hidden way into a system, to use later in the same operation or in a flashback.
- **Leads in:** Infiltration, Investigation.

### Violet
- **Standing:** middle.
- **Bred and trained for:** art, design and music. Some are Carvers, who reshape bodies.
- **In combat:** little personal fighting. Can create disguises and distractions or lift morale.
- **Out of combat:** shapes how people see things: propaganda, fashion, performance, disguise and, for Carvers, new faces and bodies.
- **Cost:** valued for their talent, but dependent on the whims of patrons.
- **Signature edge:** *Reshape.* Change how a person, a place or an idea is seen.
- **Leads in:** Gold society, Rally, Downtime.

### Orange
- **Standing:** low.
- **Bred and trained for:** mechanics and engineering.
- **In combat:** repairs armor and ships in the middle of a fight, sabotages enemy gear, rigs charges.
- **Out of combat:** fixes, builds and breaks machines. Knows how a station or ship works from the inside.
- **Cost:** low Station. Their skill is needed, but rarely thanked.
- **Signature edge:** *Field Repair.* Make broken gear work for one more scene, or make enemy gear fail at a chosen moment.
- **Leads in:** Ship battle, Infiltration, Downtime.

### Gray
- **Standing:** low, with the respect given to soldiers.
- **Bred and trained for:** soldiering and policing, in the legions and the city guards.
- **In combat:** disciplined squad tactics, firearms and teamwork. Trained above the base Might tier.
- **Out of combat:** knows security procedures from the inside, military networks and how a chain of command really works.
- **Cost:** Duty to the chain of command. Many lowColors see Grays as Society's enforcers.
- **Signature edge:** *Drill.* Coordinate with allies so a group action counts as one strong action.
- **Leads in:** Battle, Infiltration.

### Obsidian
- **Standing:** low.
- **Bred and trained for:** war. Huge and strong, raised on the polar ice and kept from learning about the wider world.
- **In combat:** the only Color that can match Golds in raw force. Top Might tier. Terrifying in a charge.
- **Out of combat:** intimidation, endurance in brutal conditions, and the traditions of their clans.
- **Cost:** often denied schooling by Society. They can never blend in. Old faiths and loyalties pull on them.
- **Signature edge:** *Terror.* Their presence breaks the nerve of enemies before the first blow.
- **Leads in:** Battle, Duel, Travel and survival.

### Brown
- **Standing:** low.
- **Bred and trained for:** service: cooking, cleaning and running households.
- **In combat:** rarely a fighter, but already inside the room. Can carry a weapon in, open a door, or poison a cup.
- **Out of combat:** Unseen in Gold spaces. Hears everything, goes into private rooms and kitchens, and belongs to networks of servants who share what they know.
- **Cost:** harsh treatment. Any highColor can punish them with little consequence.
- **Signature edge:** *Unseen.* While serving, you are not noticed unless you draw attention to yourself.
- **Leads in:** Infiltration, Gold society, Investigation.

### Pink
- **Standing:** low.
- **Bred and trained for:** companionship: beauty, pleasure, and the reading of people.
- **In combat:** rarely a fighter in the open, but deadly up close and unexpected.
- **Out of combat:** reads emotions, wins trust, learns secrets, and has access to the private lives of the powerful. Natural spies.
- **Cost:** treated as property, and the attention they receive is dangerous. Carries the weight of what they have endured.
- **Signature edge:** *Read.* Learn what a person wants, fears or hides.
- **Leads in:** Infiltration, Gold society, Investigation, Negotiation.

### Red
- **Standing:** low, the lowest Color.
- **Bred and trained for:** mining and hard labor. On Mars, the helldivers work the deepest mines.
- **In combat:** quick hands and endurance. Underestimated by everyone, and used to tight spaces and dangerous tools.
- **Out of combat:** survives harsh places, knows mines and industrial sites, and holds the trust of other lowColors through family, song and shared hardship.
- **Cost:** lowest Station. Often lied to about the world, and often denied schooling.
- **Signature edge:** *Endure.* Keep going after a blow, a wound or a shock that would stop anyone else.
- **Leads in:** Infiltration, Travel and survival, Rally.

## Carved characters

A carved character was remade by a Carver to pass as another Color, the way Darrow was.

- **Two sets of traits.** A carved character keeps the skills and bonds of their birth Color and gains the body and Station of their new Color.
- **Cover.** Every slip (a wrong word, a missing habit, a familiar face) fills an exposure clock. When it fills, someone knows the truth.
- **Cost.** The carving is painful, it takes time, and it can never be fully undone. The character belongs fully to neither world.
- **Leads in:** whatever their new Color leads in, plus Infiltration, because they can go places nobody expects someone like them to be.

How carving works at character creation is still open (O6 in [00 Decisions](00-decisions.md)).
