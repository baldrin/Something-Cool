# 00 Decisions

This is the record of what the group has decided and what is still open. When a decision is made, move it from **Open** to **Decided** and note the date.

## Decided

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | **Era: an original era of our own.** | Our story should not collide with the books' events. Every book can be used as reference. See [05 Era seeds](05-era-seeds.md). |
| D2 | **Color hierarchy: true to the books.** | Golds and Obsidians really are physically stronger. Other Colors get different edges instead of equal power. See [03 Colors](03-colors.md). |
| D3 | **Combat stays central, but it is not the only focus.** | Combat is the group's favorite part. With this many Colors (Pink, Green, Gold and the rest), the game also needs strong scenes that are not fights. |
| D4 | **Character building stays deep.** | The group loves making choices at creation and as characters grow. |
| D5 | **Top problem to solve: players with nothing to do.** | The group likes combat because everyone gets a turn and a job. The game must give every Color something to do in every kind of scene. See [01 Goals](01-goals.md). |
| D6 | **The dice core waits.** | We plan the rest first, then pick the roll that serves it best. Candidates are listed below. |
| D7 | **The design lives in this repo as markdown.** | Every change is recorded and anyone in the group can read it on GitHub. |

## Open

Each open question lists its options, a current lean if there is one, and what it depends on.

### O1. Dice core

| Option | How it works | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **d20, upgraded** | d20 + modifiers against a target number. Keeps advantage and disadvantage. Adds degrees of success and risk levels. | The group already knows it. Fine grained bonuses suit tactical combat. | A flat d20 is swingy, so experts fail often. Degrees of success need a clear rule. |
| **2d10, three outcomes** | 2d10 + stat. High result is a clean success, middle is success with a cost, low is a GM move. | Partial success is built in. Results cluster near the average, so skill matters more. | Bonuses are worth more near the middle than at the ends, which can be harder to balance. |
| **d6 pool** | Roll d6s equal to your rating and keep the highest. 6 is a success, 4 or 5 is success with a cost. | Fast and story first. Strong fit for operations and flashbacks. | Less granular, so tactical combat has fewer numbers to work with. |

**Depends on:** how tactical combat should be (O3), and how the operation structure from [04 Scene types](04-scene-types.md) works out. Before choosing, we can run a probability script for each option and compare how often experts and novices succeed.

### O2. Levels or no levels

- **Levels like 5e.** Familiar, with a clear sense of growth.
- **Advances earned by playing to your drives,** like Beliefs in Burning Wheel. Ties growth to story.
- **Hybrid.** A small number of ranks, plus advances earned from drives and bonds.

**Lean:** hybrid. The group loves building characters, and a hybrid may also avoid the late game power creep 5e is known for. (Power creep is a suggestion; the group has not named it as a problem.)

### O3. How tactical combat is

- A grid with minis, like 5e.
- Zones (a few named areas per fight) with positioning that matters.
- Theater of the mind.

**Depends on:** the group loves tactical combat, so we should keep real positioning. Zones may be the best fit for multi-front operations.

### O4. Lethality and healing

How deadly is a razor fight? Do we use hit points, wounds, or both? How much does a Yellow matter for recovery? The books are brutal, and named characters die. We need to decide how often player characters can.

### O5. How the hierarchy shows up in the rules

How large is the physical gap between a Gold or Obsidian and everyone else? How do we make "being overlooked" a real mechanical edge for lowColors? See the proposals in [03 Colors](03-colors.md).

### O6. Carving

Can a player start carved, the way Darrow was? If so, how does cover work (an exposure clock, a second set of social traits, or something else), and what does it cost?

### O7. Structure for high stakes scenes

Do we adopt the **operation** proposal from [04 Scene types](04-scene-types.md)? If so, how many fronts per scene, and how long is a round?

### O8. Shared team resource

Should the crew have one shared pool that anyone can spend to help another player, like Dune's Momentum? If so, it should be one currency, not two.

### O9. Bonds and betrayal

Loyalty and betrayal run through every book. Options include bonds that wear down under pressure (Delta Green) and influence over each other (Masks). See [02 Borrowed mechanics](02-borrowed-mechanics.md).

### O10. Character creation path

Options include Color plus a role, plus a background, or a lifepath (choose your upbringing, schooling and first posting in order, as Traveller does).

### O11. Which era

Pick from [05 Era seeds](05-era-seeds.md), or combine or invent one.

### O12. Name of the game
