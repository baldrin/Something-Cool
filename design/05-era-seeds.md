# 05 Era seeds

The group chose an era of its own (decision D1 in [00 Decisions](00-decisions.md)), so our story does not collide with events in the books. Here are five starting points. Pick one, combine two, or use them to spark a different idea.

Each seed stays at the edges of canon on purpose: places and times the books mention or imply, but do not show in detail. Anything we invent can be changed if it ever contradicts a book.

## 1. The Dawn of Colors

**When:** the early generations after the Conquering, when the Golds have taken power but the Colors are not yet fixed.

**Premise.** Society is still being built. The engineering that will divide humanity into Colors is underway, and families are being split into castes for the first time. Some people still remember being simply human. The crew is a mixed group of people who are watching the hierarchy form around them, and who will either help build it, profit from it or try to stop it.

**Spotlight:** every Color, since the castes are new and the lines between them are still blurry. Violets and Yellows matter in a special way, because they are the ones doing the shaping.

**Suits:** a resistance or tragedy campaign about how the world of the books came to be. The players know how history goes, which makes every small victory feel heavier.

## 2. The Ghosts of Triton

**When:** generations before the books, on a moon far out in the Rim.

**Premise.** An uprising on Triton came close to breaking the Society's hold on the outer moons, then vanished from the records. The crew are its fighters, smugglers and spies, working cell by cell against a garrison that does not yet know how organized they are. Canon never tells us what happened, so the ending is ours to decide.

**Spotlight:** Reds, Oranges, Greens and Blues as the uprising's core, with Grays and Obsidians as the most valuable recruits, if they can be turned.

**Suits:** an insurgency campaign of sabotage, heists and hard choices about how far to go. Operations would be mostly infiltration and battle.

## 3. The Silver Exchange

**When:** any time in the Society's long middle centuries.

**Premise.** Two Gold Houses are fighting a war without fleets. They fight through trade, debt, blackmail and assassination on Luna and Mercury, and both sides use lowColor agents they consider disposable. The crew works for one House, or for itself, in a city where every deal is a weapon and open violence is rare and decisive.

**Spotlight:** Silvers, Coppers, Pinks, Greens and Browns. Golds are the patrons and targets more than the agents.

**Suits:** an intrigue and investigation campaign. Combat is rare, short and deadly, which makes every fight a turning point.

## 4. The Fall of a House

**When:** any time, with a fictional Gold House of our own.

**Premise.** The players are one Gold House's household: the heir, their Obsidian bodyguard, a Pink confidant, a Yellow physician, a Brown steward, a Blue pilot and others. The House is losing a feud and its allies are drifting away. The household must save it, or decide whether it deserves to be saved. Because every House needs every Color, a mixed crew comes naturally.

**Spotlight:** every Color, each with a clear place in the household and a clear reason to stay or betray it.

**Suits:** a drama of loyalty and betrayal. It can also span generations, in the style of Pendragon, with players taking on heirs and successors as the House rises or falls.

## 5. The Far Survey

**When:** any time, on a ship sent beyond the known settlements.

**Premise.** A survey ship carrying every Color has gone further out than any before it, months away from the nearest Gold. Far from Society, the hierarchy aboard begins to strain: who commands when the ship's survival depends on a Red's endurance or a Blue's navigation? Then the ship finds something out there that nobody expected.

**Spotlight:** Blues, Oranges and Yellows for survival. Golds and Grays holding command together. lowColors deciding whether to keep obeying.

**Suits:** an exploration and survival campaign with a slowly building social tension. It is the farthest from canon of the five, so it has the most freedom.

## Comparing the seeds

| Seed | Main scene types | Combat level | How close to the books' world | Mixed Color crew |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Dawn of Colors | Infiltration, Rally, Battle | Medium | Medium | Natural |
| The Ghosts of Triton | Infiltration, Battle, Travel and survival | High | Medium | Mostly lowColors |
| The Silver Exchange | Gold society, Investigation, Negotiation | Low | High | Natural |
| The Fall of a House | Gold society, Duel, Court and ceremony | Medium | High | Natural |
| The Far Survey | Travel and survival, Ship battle, Rally | Medium | Low | Natural |

Since the group loves combat, **The Ghosts of Triton** and **The Fall of a House** may be the strongest starts. The first has the most fighting, and the second has the widest mix of scene types, including duels.
