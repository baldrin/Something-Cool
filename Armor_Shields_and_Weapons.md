
The Red Rising universe presents a wide array of options for dealing damage to your opponent and tanking damage from the other way. This ensures that nearly problem has a solution-- but a single solution is unlikely to solve every problem. While some of the "best" armor and weapons in the out there can dole out and take extreme punishment, less advanced and more available equipment often better suits the situation at hand

While a starShell might run riot on a battlefield, good luck clearing a heist without being noticed while clomping around in a 4m (13ft) tall, 3 ton (2700kg) behemoth. And while a Razor is the ultimate melee weapon, an Obsidian who touches one in the Society is sentenced to death by starvation, and it won't do you much good pinned down at the end of a hallway with gray sharpshooters pinning you down.

From a combat perspective, armor of the same tier takes standard damage from weapons of the same tier. Weapons below the tier of the armor it is attacking may have reduced or no effect, depending on the matchup.


## Tier 0 -- Pre-Societal

This is your normal bar room brawl level of tech. No armor. Fists, knives, bows, and thrown weapons. T0 encounters would never be seen in a modern battlefield, but could be seen in a back alley or non-combat scenario that goes south.

## Tier 1 -- DuroPlastic

This is standard issue for low level law enforcement and riot control. Places of societal unrest not rising to military deployment could potentially see T1 armor and weapons used.

Melee weapons in this tier would include knifeRings and duroPlastic shivs.
Ranged weapons in this tier would include slug guns and riot control non-lethal weaponry. 

Armor, referred to as duroArmor, at this level is designed to rebuff standard slug guns and knives aimed at vital spots. It is typically not designed to cover entire bodies and is normally used only for chest pieces and helmets.

## Tier 2 -- DuroSteel

This is the highest tier of completely unpowered equipment. DuroSteel is much stronger and lighter than standard steel and used as a standard building material of starships and light armor. It is rarely seen in real world battlefield use but does appear as the first weapons given to young Golds at the Institute. Scorchers on the other hand are ubiquitous for sidearm use by military, police, gangs, and private citizens.

Melee weapons in this tier include duroSteel blades, axes, hammers, and spears. 
Ranged weapons include the ubiquitous Scorcher.

DuroSteel armor, unlike regular duroArmor, frequently comes as full armor kits. ScarabSkin is the standout armor in this tier-- famous for its use in spec ops and stealth and speed based operations, lack of need for battery power, and frequent use and underlayment for higher tier armor.
## Tier 3 -- Ion

Ion weaponry is standard legionnaire Gray kit and is the most likely to be seen in battlefield scenarios by regular infantry. The ion generator on the weapons create a field that picks apart standard duroSteel armor and cuts through flesh like hot butter. The main drawback of this tier is that it requires power cells to fuel its ion generator. Ion weaponry has some effect against shields.

Melee weapons in this tier include ionDaggers, ionBlades, ionAxes, ionSpears.
Ranged weapons in this tier include ionArrows, ionPistols and ionRifles

IonArmor is fairly rare due to the trade-off of carrying power cells, cost, thermal signature, and lack of significant utility over that of ScarabSkin and the substantially more protective recoilArmor.
## Tier 4-- Recoil

Recoil class armor and weaponry is a definitive step up from grunt armor into elite and spec ops unit territory.

There are no T4 melee weapons as the goal of this tech class is to close the gap between mid and lowColors and Golds on the battlefield and that is best accomplished by putting range between them.
Ranged weapons in this tier are highlighted by two standouts:
recoilRifles-- repeating assault rifles designed to provide excellent armor penetration, shield burn down, and covering fire against well armored opponents
railRifles-- fire semi-automatic railSlugs through a miniaturized personnel capable railgun mechanism. These are favored among sharpshooters and snipers.

RecoilArmor is formed by interlocking recoilPlates that mold to the body. It has built in chemical and mechanical self-healing properties and can stop bleeding wounds. It does not require power cells. 

## Tier 5-- Pulse

PulseArmor is expensive, nearly indestructible, and the only armor capable of providing resistance to Razors. It is made by coupling recoilArmor with a pulseShield generator and includes joint servos for increasing speed, strength, and responsiveness. It is saved for only the heaviest hitters and not only protects, but amplifies their killing power. It is invariably custom built and fitted to the user.

A fully charged pulseArmor with shield up is the ultimate nonmechanized personal protection armor. The only downsides are its reliance on quickly used power cells, sensitivity to EMPs, and that the shield can be overloaded with enough damage.

PulseWeapons are the answer to this armor. Designed to send in high output pulse energy directly into the shield and armor, it is the only class of weaponry, aside from Razors, to match up. 

Melee weapons include pulseAxes, pulseHammers, pulseBlades, pulseSpears, and pulseShafts.
Ranged weapons include pulseRifles and pulseFists

## Tier 6-- Apex

Razors are the ultimate melee weapon in the Red Rising universe and the signature weapon and status symbol for Gold warriors. Made by specially designed polyenne fiber-- harder than diamond in its solid form-- it can slice through any other armor or, with appropriate skill, shield. Typically about a meter long, can be toggled with a biochemical reaction into a whip form that is twice as long. Pulse armor provides some, but not complete protection against razor attacks.

StarShells also belong in this category as a bridge between personal armor and outright vehicular combat. At 4 meters tall and weighing 3 tons and armed to the teeth with shoulder and gauntlet weapons and built-in gravBoots. It can turn any capable pilot into a monster. 
## Tier 7 -- Capital

Capital ship combat is inconceivably destructive at the personnel level. Even starShells paired with pulseArmor would only provide modest protection from a glancing or distant Capital attack.

Any individual hit directly with a Capital weapon is not just dead, they are completely vaporized. No level of armor aside from being inside a ship is going to protect from this level of attack.

Ships of torchShip size and larger have access to weaponry including, but not limited to, heavy cannons, missiles, full sized rail and coil guns, particle beams, and atomics. These can wreak havoc on a planetary scale or turn a moon to glass.
