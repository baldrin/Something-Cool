# Gray and the Wardog

"_Chop 'em if they're taller. Stomp 'em if they're smaller. Mauler, brawler, legacy hauler, smoke that crow, earn his holler. Mauler, brawler, legacy hauler, smoke that ant, pay off your collar. Legio! Aeterna! Victrix_!"  

## The Color: Gray

Grays are the multitool of the Society. They serve as police, infantry, spec ops, and royal guards. Of all the 14 Colors, they are presented with the seemingly the widest range of possible skills and abilities within their own caste. Grays are shown being treated as barely above lowColors in the case of lowly mine guards but, in exceptional cases, can be seen as near (but never *actual*) peers with Golds in the Society. Their social mobility is strictly tied to their military performance and rank. Underachievers are bound to end up on beat patrol in a backwater. The strong make it to be legionnaires. Only the most elite among them end up serving in a legion assigned to the Praetorian Guard-- like the legendary Legio XIII Dracones, the Dragoons.   

Of the three warColors, they are by far the most numerous, outnumbering Golds by an estimated 1000 to 1. Due to these numbers, they are the face of control and enforcement of the hierarchy and are responsible for the ordinary work of violence. A Brown on the surface of Earth may live their whole life and never see a Peerless Scarred, but they will certainly have come in contact with the many Grays enforcing the orders and customs that keep those Golds in power. Accordingly, they are frequent targets of disdain from the less privileged members of the Society. As the most visible, most numerous, and least physically terrifying of the enforcement arm of the Society, they are unlikely to make fast friends with other groups lower on the food chain.   

With this isolation comes an intense canine-like loyalty to their Color and their leaders. And, no, the attack dog metaphor isn't subtle. They refer to their training units as "kennels" and refer to their slang as "dog tongue". The signature class for this Color-- the Wardog-- fully embraces this persona: an attack dog bred for the waging of war.  

## Gray Progression

Your base Gray is already a trained militant. The realities of war are lived experiences for them and not just hypotheticals. With this training comes the know-how to effectively coordinate and work as a team, even in adverse conditions. They also know their way around a variety of weapons from the get-go.  

After moving into Tier 1, the Gray has demonstrated a particular proficiency on the battlefield in defense, tactics, or special operations. 

In Tier 2, the Gray's area of expertise is going to be hard to match. Lurchers become killers, Praetorians solidify themselves as effective tanks, and Freelancers have freedom and connections to get jobs done no one else would try.

## The Signature Class: Wardog

**Role:** Jack of all trades (striker, tank, support, control)
**Focus:** Even distribution
**Colors:** Gray only

The Wardog is the veteran who has been in every role in the legion at least twice. They shoot like a Sharpshooter, dig in like a Juggernaut, patch wounds like a Field Medic, and slip away like a Ghost. While they may not reach the ultimate peak a specialist in that area might, they are also never the wrong person to have along. Each class tier borrows from a different trade, and each borrowed skill has a ceiling the true specialist does not.  

### Discipline

Discipline is what a veteran has in place of pure talent: the trained habit of doing the necessary thing. A Wardog earns it by staying busy and staying upright.  

- **Cap.** Set by your class tier. Unspent Discipline is lost at the end of the scene.    
- **Opening.** You begin each scene with Discipline equal to your Color Rank. 
- **Gain 1** at the end of any turn in which you took a Physical, a Social, *and* a Mental action. **Gain 1** when an ally succeeds on a roll that your Coordinate Attack, Assist, or stim improved (once per round). **Gain 1** when you take a Hit and are still standing (once per round).  
- **Share the Load** *(Free, 1 Discipline).* An ally who can see or hear you rerolls a failed Endure or Steel Nerves.  
- **Spend** it on your skills.

### Tier 1: Dogface

- [[Aim]] *(Mental action, capped skill).* You can use your Mental action to focus Aim, lowering the threshold to critical hit by 2. **This effect stacks to a maximum reduction of 4 ( which increases to 6, at Tier 4)**. The stacks reset to zero on any attempted attack, dodge, or movement. If under the effects of [[Link]], the effect is doubled with each attempt to the same maximum critical hit threshold reduction. 
- **Field Dressing** *(Mental action, 1 Discipline).* When you Stabilize an ally, they also regain 1d6 + your INT HP.
- **Old Soldier's Eye** *(Utility).* On sight, you can name the unit, the training and the standard loadout of any Society military force, and tell which of them is in charge. You have advantage on detecting false statements regarding fallacious claims of prior military service.

### Tier 2: Trooper

- **Dig In** *(Physical action, 1 Discipline).* You Brace and until the start of your next turn you also reduce the damage of each Hit you take by your CON. You cannot dodge while dug in.
- **Slip Away** *(Physical action, 1 Discipline).* If you are in cover or Obscured, you break line of sight and become hidden.
- **Scrounge** *(Utility, 2 Discipline).* You find one piece of standard military equipment of T3 or lower that the party failed to bring.

### Tier 3: Veteran

- **Fire and Maneuver** *(Passive).* When you provide suppressive fire on a zone, one ally may immediately move up to half their speed for free during your turn. If the enemy is Pinned by you during the ally's turn, that ally's attacks against Pinned enemies gain +2 to Hit.
- **Combat Stims** *(Mental action, 1 Discipline).* Administer a stim to yourself or an adjacent ally. Stim and Crash rules apply, and the recipient also has Advantage on their next roll.
- **Packed for That** *(Utility, 2 Discipline).* You produce one mundane tool or consumable that you could have plausibly carried. Potential examples: rope, a breaching charge, a rebreather, a spare power cell, etc.

### Tier 4: Warfighter

- [[Headshot]] *(Physical action, 1 Discipline).* You attempt a ranged attack targeting the head of your opponent. The critical threshold rises by 2, but a hit adds an additional 1d12. If you make a Critical Hit, that is doubled to 2d12.
- **Breach and Clear** *(Physical action, 1 Discipline).* Make a STR Breach against a door, window, hatch, or light wall. If it gives, you may move through and make one attack. The first enemy you attack this way is Pressured whether or not you hit.
- **Friends in Low Places** *(Utility, 3 Discipline).* Wherever Grays are garrisoned, you find a friend there. This person will be willing to look the other way or help you with minor favors, like passing on patrol maps or unlocking a door for you. This cannot be used to commit obviously treasonous acts against the state.

### Tier 5: Dog of War

- **One More Time!** *(Free, 2 Discipline, once per turn).* You take one additional action this turn, from any Aspect you choose.
- **Old Dog New Tricks** *(1 Discipline, Utility, once per round).* When you fail a roll with any Wardog skill, you may reroll it.
- **The Fixer** *(Utility, 4 Discipline).* If the party has a problem, you have a connection with a solution. Name a need: a ship off-world tonight, a surgeon who doesn't ask questions, a safehouse off the grid, a carver who can get your party quick disguises. Roll d20 + LOY against a DC set by the GM (based on feasibility of the request), and on a success the arrangement is made.

### Playing a Wardog

Look at what the party lacks and be that. With no healer, you are the medic. With no tank, Dig In at the door. Your strength is that every one of your three actions each turn can be useful, and you *need* to find uses for them to generate Discipline. You are the flexibility that lets the party bend to new developments without breaking on the spot.
