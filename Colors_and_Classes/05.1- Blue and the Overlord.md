# Blue and the Overlord

*Blues are never alone, but they're seldom entirely in the room with us either.*

## The Color: Blue

Saying that Blues crew the ships of the Solar System would be understating it. If the duroSteel frames and the nanoSteel windows are a ship's skin and bones, Blues are the mind and the soul. 

While their bodies are slight, their psyche have been molded to handle the hardships of the long and lonely journeys of interplanetary travel. They are born into communal sects and undergo neurodevelopmental sculpting with neural implants and digiTats as children, allowing their minds to sync into the Synaptic Drift connecting them to their ships and to each other. They spend most of their time in this state, where their minds are so closely in contact with one another that some sense of self is surrendered to the whole. Blues experiencing the loss of a vessel or synced crewmate frequently describe the loss as akin to an amputation. Accordingly they are profoundly connected to their sect and crewmates, though there is evidence of division amongst the various sects within the Color itself.

Since they spend so much time in the Drift, communicating faster than speech ever could, they are somewhat flatfooted when interacting in physical space. They are physically frail and lacking in strength.  Their speech is often slow and lacking appropriate tone and personality and frequently have difficulty understanding social arrangements and etiquette. However, that isn't to say that they are simple. Most adult Blues already hold multiple PhDs in fields like astrophysics, orbital mechanics, and quantum physics. Many adorn themselves with tattoos of mathematical or physic equations and notations.

Blues are the obvious choice for players who prefer to assign targets and manage cycling augments for the party. 

## Blue Progression

As a Blue progresses to Tier 1 they choose which Deck of a ship they were trained on, gaining specific traits related to their background training. Each station gives a proficiency with the related background. The background gives proficiency with designated smaller vehicles such as ripWings, Wasps, and thunderWings).

When a Blue progresses to Tier 2 they choose a Station which gives them further specialized traits to synergize with their chosen class. These Stations also give advantages on piloting certain capital ships with significant advantages when doing so.

## Signature Class: Overlord

**Role:** Support and drone striker
**Focus:** INT, PER
**Colors:** Blue exclusive

The Overlord is the eye in the sky and on the ground for the party. They use support skills to share PER and help acquire targets for all party members, boosting the ability of party members to score a hit or identify foes behind cover. They limit their battlefield exposure by deploying drones to do the heavy lifting, the fighting, the shielding, and the mending.

The class starts modestly, increasing party PER, marking enemies, and having access to one level 1 drone. 

Early in class progression, your abilities are limited by the bottleneck of your Mental action and Bandwidth, restricting you to choosing between [[Link]], [[Calculated Vector]], [[Drone Swarm]], or controlling your drone(s) each turn. At Tier 3, that bottle neck eases and you can use multiple skills in the same turn by expending Social actions instead.

With this class designed for rear echelon work, Melee/Ranged/Armor (M/R/A) progression in this class is limited. ScarabSkin is both the ceiling and preferred armor throughout all tiers. Melee weapons are limited to T1 throughout progression and ranged weapons top out at T3 (ionRifles). 

## Class Resource: Bandwidth

While a Blue's mind has been optimized to work in the sync, they can still hold only so many channels open at once. Bandwidth is the representation of that capacity. 

Points of Bandwidth are stored and committed to certain skills, but those points return to the pool when that skill is turned off. As you progress in your Color and Class, this pool broadens, enabling more simultaneous skill options.

- **Capacity.** Determined by your Class Tier table plus your Color Rank.
- **Commit.** Skills hold Bandwidth while they are active. When you end a skill, you lose the effect of that skill, and the Bandwidth is free to use at once. You may drop any skill as a Free action.
- **Burn.** When one of your drones is destroyed, you lose Bandwidth equal to its level by the feedback. Burned Bandwidth cannot be committed until treated. All of it returns in Downtime.
- **Resync** *(Mental action).* You recover Burned Bandwidth equal to your INT (minimum 1). 
- **Overreach.** You may commit up to 2 Bandwidth beyond your capacity. Each point over costs you 1 Fatigue at the end of each of your turns.
- **Jammed.** While you are [Cut Off](Status%20Conditions#Cut%20Off.md) or your gear is [Shorted](Status%20Conditions.md), half your Bandwidth capacity is unavailable, and you must drop active skills until you have enough Bandwidth to run them.

### Drone Swarm

Holds 1 Bandwidth per level of skill used.

Once per scene, as a Mental action, the player can deploy Drones which act as technological familiars. Drones can be deployed based in number and strength based on Drone Swarm level (described below). Drones can take actions on the player's turn and all drone actions are encompassed by one Mental action each turn (e.g. one Mental action can give orders to 4 drones at once).

The level and number of the drones you make is always the level of Drone Swarm +1. These can be balanced in any combination. Drone Swarm Level is the same level as your Class Tier and maxes at level 4.

Ds- Level of Drone Swarm
Nd- Number of Drones
Ld- Level of Drones

Ds + 1 = Nd + Ld, where Nd ≥ 1 and Ld ≥ 1.

# Class Tiers
### Tier 1: Spotter

- [[Link]] *(Mental action, holds 1).* You connect to the party's visors or to a vehicle. Linked allies share the highest PER, anything one sees all can target, and you may apply Marked to one enemy each turn.
- [[Drone Swarm]] *(Mental action, holds Bandwidth equal to the level deployed).* Once per scene you deploy your drones, at your full Drone Swarm level or at any lower level you choose. Ordering any or all of them takes a single Mental action per turn.
- **Recon Sweep** *(Utility).* Before a scene begins, a temporary Spotter drone maps the area. The party starts with a map of the area and cannot be surprised from any approach the drone could see.

### Tier 2: Controller

- [[Calculated Vector]] *(Mental action, holds 1 until used).* Give your INT as a bonus to one ally's next dodge or ranged attack.
- **Eyes Everywhere** *(Utility, holds 1).* Outside of combat, you may make PER checks from any drone's position, and a drone can follow a target for you. Roll your d20 + PER against their d20 + PER to stay unseen.

### Tier 3: Swarm Warden

- **Split Attention** *(Passive).* While your Link is active, you may order your drones or use Link or Calculated Vector with your Social action instead of your Mental action.
- **Remote Presence** *(Utility, holds 2).* In a Scene you may contribute to a second Front through your drones while remaining on your own. Rolls made on the remote Front have disadvantage.

### Tier 4: Overwatch

- **Firing Solution** *(Social action, holds 2 until your next turn).* Choose one Marked enemy. Until the start of your next turn, every linked ally's ranged attack against that enemy has its critical threshold lowered by 2. This stacks with [[Aim]].
- **Kill Box** *(Utility, holds 1 while the position is registered).* With ten minutes to prepare a position, you register its characteristics and approaches. Enemies who enter the area are Marked automatically on arrival, with no action from you.

### Tier 5: Overlord

- **God's Eye** *(Mental action, holds 4).* For three rounds, every linked ally has advantage on attacks and dodges against Marked enemies, and the Obscured condition gives enemies no protection from them. When it ends, 2 of that Bandwidth is Burned.
- **Apex Frame** *(Passive).* A level 4 drone can be a hybrid of any two types at once. It has all the skills of both types. If there is conflict in stats, take the higher.
- **Fleet Uplink** *(Utility, holds 3).* You can extend your Link into a ship in orbit or a fleet sensor net. While you hold the uplink, its sensors reveal every enemy position on one Front. While under Fleet Uplink may also use Danger Close to call for one strike of Capital weaponry on a Marked target in the open. The strike ends the uplink and Burns all 3 Bandwidth. Danger Close may be unavailable in situations where orbital shielding is in place or if a ship is not available for target acquisition. 

### Synergies

You make other people lethal. A Wardog under Link, Calculated Vector, and Firing Solution lands hits and critical hits at rates they cannot accomplish alone. An appropriately leveled Overlord buffing two or three specially equipped Wardogs is one of the only semi-reliable strategies for taking down a pulseArmor-laden Gold at range via a Flavinian Armorkill
