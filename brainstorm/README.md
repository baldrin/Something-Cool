# Brainstorm

**Brainstorm only.** These are ideas for what each Color could do in play. None of it is a rule or a decision. The real design lives in [design/](../design/), and an idea only moves there once the group agrees on it.

## The goal of these notes

We wanted abilities that are useful in many situations and that play differently from anything in D&D. They should push players to find unusual solutions to problems and fights. We didn't hold ourselves to how other RPGs frame skills, abilities or actions.

## How to read the entries

- **Out of combat** and **In combat** list the abilities.
- **Resource** is what limits the Color's power.
- **Weaknesses** are where the Color struggles.
- **Developed further** sections go deeper on the ideas we liked most.
- Ability names, tokens and cards are working names.
