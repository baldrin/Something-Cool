# Ideas for every Color

**Brainstorm only.** Nothing here is decided. See [the brainstorm index](README.md).

These ideas came out of the single Color brainstorms and could apply to the whole game.

## One GM question each

Once per scene, ask your Color's question about a person, group, place or thing. The GM must answer truthfully and specifically. You ask it out loud, so the whole crew learns the answer.

| Color | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Gold | Where will they break? |
| Silver | What would they trade for it? |
| White | What binds them? |
| Copper | What does the record say, and what's missing? |
| Blue | Where will it be when we get there? |
| Yellow | What's wrong with them? |
| Green | What is this connected to? |
| Violet | What do they want to believe? |
| Orange | How does this break? |
| Gray | What are their orders? |
| Obsidian | Who here is afraid? |
| Brown | What do the servants know? |
| Pink | What are they hiding? |
| Red | Who here is on our side, even if they can't show it? |
| Carved | Does anyone here see through me? |

## Layers of the game

Each Color works mainly on one layer.

| Layer | What it covers | Main Colors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Force | Bodies, weapons, terrain | Gold, Obsidian, Gray |
| Rules | Law, honor, oaths, procedure | White, Copper |
| Value | Money, debt, price, risk | Silver |
| Systems | Machines, networks, ships | Green, Orange, Blue |
| Bodies and minds | Health, drugs, fear, memory | Yellow |
| People | Desire, secrets, loyalty, rumor | Pink, Brown, Red |
| Perception | Image, story, belief | Violet |
| Access (proposed) | Where you are and what you can touch | Brown |

**How it could work:** every important obstacle has a strength on each layer, and the GM questions reveal where it is weak.

Take an enemy champion before a duel:

- **Force:** very strong.
- **Rules:** he must accept any challenge from an equal.
- **Value:** he is deep in debt.
- **Bodies and minds:** he has an old knee injury.
- **People:** he fears his father.
- **Perception:** the crowd loves him.

**Layered pressure:** when two players hit the same obstacle on different layers in the same round, they get a bonus. A crew of five Golds attacks the champion's strongest layer five times, while a mixed crew attacks three weak ones. This rewards exactly the mixed crews we want.

## Table artifacts

These are physical things at the table.

| Color | Artifacts |
| --- | --- |
| White | A public Precedent deck in the middle of the table, folded Confession notes, Counsel tokens, and Oath cards from Witness |
| Silver | Policy cards and Contract cards |
| Copper | Form cards |
| Pink | Tell cards, Persona cards and Whisper cards |
| Brown | Servant tokens placed on the map |
| Green | Back door tokens placed on the map |
| Gray | An orders card the GM hands over when you ask your question |
| Orange | Fault tokens placed on enemy gear |
| Obsidian | Dread tokens placed on enemies |
| Violet | Rumor cards that get passed around the table |

**Rule of thumb:** an artifact has to be quick to use and has to involve more than one player. If it slows play down, it is not worth having.

## What to watch out for

- **GM prep.** Confession notes and layer strengths take preparation. A prompt deck for each would help.
- **Clutter.** Too many cards and tokens at once will slow the table down. We'd want to cap how many are in play.
